SituSnap Privacy Policy

Who operates SituSnap

SituSnap is the app and service name. It is published on Google Play under the developer name “situsnap” and operated by its founder and administrator. For privacy questions or requests, contact situsnap1@gmail.com.

Information SituSnap uses

Depending on the features you use, SituSnap accesses information you enter about a property, asset or field location, including postcodes, names, address details, observed-by choices and handover notes; photos you take or select; and your device’s precise location when you use visit or pin-verification features. Online services may also receive connection details such as your IP address, request time and technical device information.

The app does not require an account. This release contains no advertising or analytics SDK and does not access the advertising ID.

Why we use information and lawful basis

SituSnap uses information on your device to provide the field-record, photo and visit-verification features you choose to use. Where UK data-protection law applies, the operator relies on legitimate interests in providing the requested lookup and field-record service, maintaining its reliability, and responding to support enquiries. Online destination queries are sent only when you use a lookup feature. You can contact us to object to processing based on legitimate interests.

Records, photos and location on your device

In this release, field records, notes, photos and visit-location details are stored in the app on your device. SituSnap does not provide shared company storage or photo synchronisation. Your records remain in the app until you remove them or clear the app’s data.

Android backup may copy app data to a device or account backup, depending on your settings. Deleting a record in SituSnap does not necessarily delete a backup copy. Manage Android backup and restore through your device or account settings. Photos saved separately outside SituSnap must be removed separately.

Content you add

Photos, notes and handover details you add to SituSnap are user-provided content. You are responsible for making sure you have the right to capture and include that material, and that it is accurate and appropriate for the task. SituSnap does not independently review or verify user-provided content. In this release, field records, photos and notes stay on your device and are not uploaded to shared SituSnap storage. This does not change the privacy rights of anyone whose information appears in that content, or SituSnap’s responsibilities for the limited processing described in this policy.

Online searches, navigation and updates

When you search for a destination, the query may be sent to:

  • Breadcrumbs, SituSnap’s lookup service hosted on Cloudflare;
  • Postcodes.io, for postcode lookup; and
  • OpenStreetMap Overpass services, for mapped streets and properties.

If optional Google destination lookup is configured and used, the address query is sent to Google. When you choose to open a map or navigation, the destination address or coordinates are sent to Google Maps or the mapping service you choose. The external map provider may also process your device location and route while you navigate.

SituSnap checks online release information hosted by GitHub. Search, map and update providers receive the technical connection information needed to serve those requests. Providers may retain request logs under their own policies and may process information outside the UK. Their handling is governed by their own privacy information: Cloudflare, Postcodes.io, OpenStreetMap Foundation, Google and GitHub.

How information is shared

SituSnap uses local records only to provide features on your device. This release does not upload your field records, notes or photos to shared SituSnap storage. Destination queries go to the lookup provider used for that search, and a destination is sent to a map provider only when you choose that option. SituSnap does not sell field records or photos.

Retention and security

Local records are kept until you remove them or clear the app’s data, subject to any Android backups described above. Support emails are kept only as needed to respond to and manage your enquiry. Online service providers may retain request logs according to their own policies.

Online requests in the checked release use HTTPS. Records stored on your device are subject to your device’s security and backup settings. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices and privacy rights

You can manage camera and location permissions in Android settings. Some features may not work if you deny a permission or do not have an internet connection. Depending on the circumstances, UK data-protection rights may include access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection and data portability. To make a request, contact situsnap1@gmail.com. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office.

Photos and safe use

Take photos in public places, or on private land only where you own the land or have permission to access and photograph it. Avoid including people, vehicle registration plates or private interiors unless necessary for the field task. Use SituSnap only when it is safe to do so; do not interact with it while driving.

Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when the app’s data handling changes. The latest version will be available at this page and from SituSnap’s More section.
