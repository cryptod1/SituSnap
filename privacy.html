Information SituSnap uses

SituSnap lets you search for a destination, record a property or field asset, add notes and photos, verify a visit using your phone’s location, and open an external map. The checked Play build does not require an account and includes no advertising or analytics SDK.

  • Information you enter: postcode, property or asset name, street or address details, observed-by choice and handover notes.
  • Photos and files: images you take or choose for a field record.
  • Device location: when you use location or pin-verification features, the app reads the phone’s GPS location to help check a visit and show distance from the selected destination.
  • Connection information: when the app contacts an online service, that service may receive technical information such as your IP address, request time, and device or browser information.

On-device records, photos and location

In the checked Play release, field records, notes, photos, and visit-location details are designed to be stored in the app on your device. Shared company storage and photo synchronisation are disabled. SituSnap does not provide an in-app shared workspace in this release.

Records remain in the app until you remove them. The app’s removal control removes the saved record and its offline photo cache from the phone. Photos you separately save outside SituSnap must be removed separately. Android device or account backup settings may create or retain copies of app data; SituSnap does not control those backups. This backup and deletion description must be rechecked against the final release before publication.

Online searches and external maps

Destination searches need an internet connection. Depending on the feature used, the postcode, property or street query may be sent to:

  • Breadcrumbs, the SituSnap lookup service hosted on Cloudflare, to resolve property and address details;
  • Postcodes.io, for postcode lookup; and
  • OpenStreetMap services, including Nominatim and Overpass, for address and map-feature search.

If Google destination lookup is configured and used, the address query is sent to Google. When you choose to open navigation or a map search, the destination address or coordinates are passed to Google Maps or the mapping service you choose. The external map provider may also process your device location and route while you navigate. Each provider handles its requests under its own privacy terms.

SituSnap also checks online release information hosted by GitHub. These requests can expose connection information to GitHub.

How information is used and shared

SituSnap uses locally stored information to provide its field-record and visit-verification features. It sends destination queries to the lookup services listed above and sends a destination to an external map only when you choose that option. It does not sell field records or photos, and this release does not upload them to shared SituSnap storage.

Online providers process requests under their own privacy policies. Their retention periods, hosting locations, and international-transfer arrangements still need to be confirmed for the final policy.

Security and retention

Online service requests in the checked build use HTTPS. Field records are stored by the app on your device and are subject to the security and backup settings of that device. Records are kept until you remove them or remove the app’s data. Copies held in Android backups or outside the app may remain under the relevant provider’s retention controls.

Your choices and privacy requests

You can manage camera and location permissions in Android settings. Some capture, search, or verification features may not work without the relevant permission or an internet connection. For a privacy question or request, contact situsnap1@gmail.com. UK users may also contact the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

Photos and safe use

Take photos in public places, or on private land only where you own the land or have permission to access and photograph it. Avoid including people, vehicle registration plates, or private interiors unless necessary for the field task.
