Privacy at SituSnap

We are preparing SituSnap for a controlled company pilot. This page explains the current preview and will be expanded with the final service details before public release.

Pre-release noticeThe preview app keeps records on the device. Its company cloud upload and shared-record access are disabled while the access controls are being built and tested. Please do not enter live company or personal evidence in this preview.

This website

This preview website is informational. It has no sign-up form, photo upload, record lookup, or analytics code.

App data

SituSnap is designed to help field teams record locations, notes, photos and status updates. Location and camera access are used only when the relevant app features are used. The preview build stores its working records locally on the device. Cloud syncing is disabled.

Before team release

Before the company service opens, we will publish the responsible organisation and contact details, the data collected, the storage and service providers, retention and deletion periods, access arrangements, and how to exercise privacy rights. The app will not be opened for live company records until those details and the access controls are ready.

Questions

If you are part of the company pilot, please contact your SituSnap administrator through your usual company channel.
