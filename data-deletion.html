SituSnapData deletion

Effective 8 October 2026 · SituSnap 1.3.0

Request deletion of your SituSnap data

To request deletion, email situsnap1@gmail.com with the subject “SituSnap data deletion request.”

SituSnap does not require an account. Tell us what information or support correspondence you want deleted and provide only the details needed to identify it. Do not email photos or sensitive information unless we ask and it is necessary.

Delete records saved in the app

  1. To remove one saved verified location, open its record in SituSnap, choose Remove verified location, and confirm.
  2. To erase all SituSnap data stored by the app on this device, open Android Settings → Apps → SituSnap → Storage and choose Clear storage or the equivalent. Menu names vary by device. Uninstalling the app also removes its on-device data.

Removing a verified location deletes its saved record, address and postcode details, handover notes, saved pin, local photo and cached photo from the app’s stores on this device.

What may remain after removing a record

To stop a removed location from reappearing, SituSnap may keep a small removal marker on the device containing the record identifier and a postcode/address match. It remains only in the app’s local data and is removed when you clear the app’s storage or uninstall it. In this release, field records, photos and these markers are not uploaded to shared SituSnap storage.

Android device or account backups may contain an earlier copy of app data. Removing a record or uninstalling the app does not necessarily erase backup copies; review your Android or Google backup settings and remove any copy there. Photos or files saved separately outside SituSnap must also be deleted separately.

Information handled by other services

When you use a destination lookup, the query is sent to the relevant lookup service. If you choose map navigation, the destination is sent to the mapping service you selected. These providers may retain request and connection logs, such as a query, IP address or request time, under their own policies. SituSnap cannot erase a provider’s independent logs; contact the provider directly to ask about its deletion process.

Support email correspondence is kept only as long as needed to respond to and manage the enquiry, subject to any legal obligations. You can request deletion of correspondence by emailing the address above.

For details of the providers and data SituSnap uses, read the SituSnap Privacy Policy.
